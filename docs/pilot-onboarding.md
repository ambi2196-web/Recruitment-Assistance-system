# Requirement Desk: pilot guide

For the 1 recruiter and 1 stakeholder in the pilot's first wave. Reading time: 5 minutes.

## What the desk is

A helper that sits beside Conrep. **The AI drafts. You decide.** Every AI text says "Draft — check before use". Check it before you act on it.

## What the AI never does

- It never sees client names, rates, or candidate names and contact details. The desk removes them first.
- It never sends anything to a client or a candidate. You send everything yourself.
- It never scores or ranks a person. It shows evidence, and you judge.

## If you are the recruiter

1. **A new client requirement arrives.** Forward it to the desk mailbox, or paste it under **Forward a client email**.
2. **Read the brief.** It explains the project, the tools, similar job titles and LinkedIn Recruiter filters.
3. **If a line is wrong, click "Wrong"** and say why in a few words. This is how the desk gets better.
4. **Use the screening kit** on calls. Its identity checks help spot proxy interviews.
5. **After a call, click "Add a candidate".** Upload the resume (PDF or Word) or paste it, then add your call notes.
6. **Mark every flag** as Explained, Confirmed or Unresolved, with a short note. Then approve.
7. **Copy the Conrep text** into Conrep.
8. **Check outcomes** under **My submissions**.

## If you are the stakeholder

1. **A requirement check arrives.** Choose one:
   - **SEND:** copy the questions and send them to the client from your own mailbox.
   - **EDIT:** change the questions, then send them yourself.
   - **SKIP:** choose a reason. The recruiter will source on assumption.
2. **When the client answers,** paste the answer into the desk, or reply to the desk's email and keep `[REQ-n]` in the subject.
3. **Review each submission.** Read any red, unresolved flags first. Then either forward it to the client yourself and click **I forwarded this to the client**, or reject it with a reason.
4. **When the client replies,** click one button: Interview, Selected, Rejected or No reply yet. The desk reminds you on day 3 and day 7.

## If something goes wrong

- **The desk is down or slow:** go back to your usual way of working. Nothing in the desk is needed to submit a candidate.
- **You see a client name, rate or candidate detail where it should be hidden:** stop, and tell the owner the same day.
- **The AI says something wrong:** click **Wrong**, or tell the owner.

## Each week

The owner will ask you for 15 minutes of feedback. Notes go in `docs/pilot-notes.md`.
